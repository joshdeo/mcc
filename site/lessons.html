Lessons and topics

Every lesson opens a free Lichess study. Live lessons run on Monday and Friday sessions only, from 6:45 to 7:15 pm and 8:00 to 8:30 pm.

About these studiesStudies are free and open on Lichess.org, and each one opens in a new tab. Only a few are annotated so far, and the club plans to add more comments over time. Dashed topics do not have a published link yet.
