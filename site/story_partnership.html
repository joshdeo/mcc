MCC expands through a new partnership

A formal collaboration with The Erindale Academy adds Tuesday and Thursday sessions for players on the waitlist.

Oct 2, 2026
PartnerThe Erindale Academy
New sessionsTuesday and Thursday, starting October 2026
Written byAnna Pan

Demand for chess at the club has been higher than its space since 2024, which is why programs are often full and a waitlist exists. To help, MCC is partnering formally with The Erindale Academy to run extra sessions on Tuesdays and Thursdays.

The aim is to bring in players who have been waiting, whether they want enrichment, competitive training or a way to build thinking skills. Both organizations share a belief in strategic thinking and reflective decision making.

See the schedule for the Tuesday and Thursday tournaments, or join the waitlist.
