Rules and regulations

A plain language summary. The official rules document is linked below.

Conduct

Be respectful. No staring, coughing or other psychological tactics, and no verbal abuse or deliberate provoking. Shake hands before and after games and accept a loss graciously.

Registration

Sign in at reception and pay any event fee before you play.

Equipment and space

Treat sets, boards and clocks with care. Leave the playing area the way you found it.

Phones and devices

Turn cell phones off when you enter the playing area. Media players and handheld games are not allowed.

Smoking and alcohol

Smoking and alcohol are strictly forbidden, and nobody may enter the club under the influence of alcohol.

Discipline

Rule breaks bring a verbal warning. After two warnings, further violations may lead to expulsion.

Tournament rules

Tournaments follow the FIDE Official Rules of Chess. Club arbiters settle disputes and their rulings are final.

Parents and bullying

Parents are responsible for their children. Bullying is not tolerated. Bring concerns to a club officer directly.

Tiebreaks and byes

Tiebreaks, in order
  1. Head to head
  2. Buchholz Cut 1
  3. Buchholz
Byes

Monday and Friday juniors: no byes. If you are absent, you do not score a point.

Wednesday: request up to 2 half point byes in rounds 1 to 3. We must receive your request before the round starts.
