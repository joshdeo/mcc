Questions parents ask

Is my child ready?

Kids should know how the pieces move before joining. If they are new to the game, start at ChessKid.com first, then come play.

What happens on a club night?

At the kids club on Mondays and Fridays, kids play a few games each night. Informal games run throughout, with rated tournament matches around 7:30 pm.

Is there homework?

No, not really. If your child wants more practice, ChessKid.com suits kids under 13 and Chess.com suits ages 13 and up.

Where do we start with notation and etiquette?

The club recommends this Lichess study on notation and etiquette, credited to Coach Ethan. More lessons are on the lessons page.
