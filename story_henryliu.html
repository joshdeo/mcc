NM Henry Liu’s MCC story

From beginner at the original campus club to National Master, coach and webmaster.

Aug 5, 2024
TitlesNational Candidate Master (2019), National Master
Club rolesJunior coach, webmaster, developer

Henry Liu started as a beginner at the club’s original home on the University of Toronto Mississauga campus, drawn in by a welcoming room full of players at every level.

He earned the National Candidate Master title in 2019 and later became a National Master. After his first title he started giving back as a junior coach on Monday evenings.

During the pandemic he led the creation of an online MCC club on Chess.com and helped run more than 200 virtual tournaments. When the club reopened he became webmaster and developer, and launched the club’s new website in February 2024.

Chess, he says, teaches resilience, how to face obstacles and how to manage your own performance, lessons that reach well beyond the board.
