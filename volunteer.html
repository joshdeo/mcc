Volunteer with us

Help run the largest chess club in Canada. No resume needed, and no chess knowledge needed either.

What volunteers do

Set up and put away equipment, help kids and parents, and play with kids who are new to chess. It is a great way to earn volunteer hours, especially if chess is an interest.

Getting started

High school students can earn hours toward the 40 hour requirement. New to chess? We can teach you. All volunteers are welcome, new volunteers and students included.
